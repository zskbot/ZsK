The company

Software is too complex to be reviewed by surface alone.

ZsK-bot is an engineering-focused AI initiative built around one idea: software intelligence should understand the context behind a change, not simply recognize patterns inside a file.

Our systems are designed to work alongside developers — analyzing changes, surfacing risk, explaining findings and helping engineering teams make better technical decisions.

ZsK-bot is not a single prompt wrapped around an API. It is a modular analysis system where review, routing, model providers and engineering context can evolve independently.

Architecture

From change
to insight.

The architecture separates the review engine from model providers and delivery surfaces. That separation is what allows the system to evolve without locking the product to one integration.
